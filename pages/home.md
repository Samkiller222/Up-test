intro: Five phases from your employer starting the application to collecting your card.
phases:
  - title: Apply and pay
    summary: Your employer starts, you finish, and the fee is paid
    steps:
      - heading: Your employer starts the Single Permit application.
        text: They fill in their part on the Identità Single Permit Portal and send it to you.
      - heading: Add your details.
        text: You will add your details and upload it.
      - heading: Pay the fee.
        text: Either you or your employer can pay the application fee.
      - heading: Your employer submits it.
        text: The employer will check everything and submit it.
  - title: Application vetting
    summary: Your documents are checked and you're invited for biometrics
    steps:
      - heading: Identità checks your documents.
        text: If something is missing, it comes back to you and your employer to fix.
      - heading: Get your invitation letter.
        text: Once everything is in order, your application is registered and you will be sent a biometrics invitation letter.
  - title: Visa application centre
    summary: Book, attend, and give your biometrics
    steps:
      - heading: Book your appointment.
        text: Use your invitation letter to book at a [VFS centre](https://identita.gov.mt/central-visa-unit-visa-extended-service-branches/).
      - heading: Go to your appointment.
        text: At this appointment you will submit your biometrics, and these will be sent on for your card. Some people may be asked to do an interview on that same day.
  - title: Conditional approval
    summary: Background checks, then conditional approval
    steps:
      - heading: Final checks.
        text: Identità will conduct their final checks. If all is approved, you will receive a conditional approval by email.
      - heading: Get your conditional approval.
        text: If you registered for a visa, it will be issued and you collect it from the VFS centre where you submitted your biometrics.
  - title: Arrive in Malta and get your card
    summary: Travel, finish your documents, and collect your card
    steps:
      - heading: Travel to Malta.
        text: In Malta you will be requested to upload any extra documents required online within 2 months of this notice.
      - heading: Book your card collection.
        text: If the submitted extra documents are approved, you will be instructed to book an appointment for your card collection. These instructions will be mailed to you.
      - heading: Collect your card.
        text: Bring your original documents.
